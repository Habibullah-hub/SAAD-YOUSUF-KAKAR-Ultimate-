ABOUT THE PLATFORM

Your digital world, one destination.

SK Cyber Hub is an independent digital discovery project created to make useful technology easier to find for people in Pakistan, Saudi Arabia, Urdu-speaking communities and, over time, a worldwide audience.

What we are building

  • AI tools and free online utilities
  • Android apps and trustworthy software resources
  • Documents, learning resources and games
  • Technology coverage and links to international news publishers
  • SK Pulse community for member posts, photo sharing, follows, likes, comments and saved posts

Our standards

We aim to link to official publishers where possible, identify external destinations clearly, respect copyright and software licences, and avoid misleading download buttons. Free plans and features can change, so users should check each provider's current terms.

Our growth plan

We are starting with a lean, low-cost directory and plan to improve it in stages: stronger search, Urdu and Arabic support, curated resources, accessible design, reliable publishing workflows and useful original tools.

SK Cyber Hub is independent and is not affiliated with Microsoft, Google, Apple or the publishers linked from this website.
