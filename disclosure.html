TRANSPARENCY

Advertising, Affiliate & Sponsorship Disclosure

Last updated: 3 November 2026

SYK Cyber Hub intends to support its free resources through lawful, clearly disclosed revenue streams. At the time of this publication, advertising placements, affiliate tracking links and paid sponsorships are not yet active on this website.

Future advertising

If advertisements are introduced, they will be distinguishable from editorial content and ordinary resource links. Advertising will not be presented as an official download button or as an endorsement by a software publisher.

Future affiliate links

Some links may become affiliate links in the future. Where that happens, the link or nearby content will be clearly labelled. SYK Cyber Hub may earn a commission if a visitor makes a qualifying purchase, usually at no additional cost to the visitor. Affiliate relationships will not change the need to identify useful, legitimate resources honestly.

How the platform may earn

SYK Cyber Hub does not earn money merely because a visitor consumes mobile data or internet bandwidth. We will not secretly resell visitor bandwidth or use visitors' devices as proxy or relay nodes. Possible future revenue sources include clearly labelled advertisements, affiliate commissions, disclosed sponsorships and optional creator features. Visitors will not be charged by SYK Cyber Hub to browse the free directory or community. Any future paid feature or creator payout will be described before activation.

Sponsorships

Sponsored placements and paid partnerships will be labelled as sponsored. We will not knowingly promote illegal, deceptive, counterfeit or unsafe products and services.

External downloads

External links lead to third-party websites. Their availability, pricing, download process and terms are controlled by those providers. Always verify the publisher and requested permissions before installing software.
