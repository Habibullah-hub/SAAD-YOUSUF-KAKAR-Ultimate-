Policy baseline · Updated 27 November 2026

1. Who this applies to

These rules apply to material submitted to SYK-controlled areas: member profiles, community posts, comments, creator submissions, public video listings, resource listings, reviews, messages where moderation is legally and technically permitted, and material recommended or promoted by SYK. External websites and embedded services remain governed by their own terms and policies.

Visitors

May browse public areas and must not attack, scrape abusively, bypass access controls or disrupt the service.

Members

May use member features, post lawful material, protect other people's privacy and follow the community rules.

Creators & publishers

Must own or have permission for submitted material, provide accurate descriptions and follow content and monetization rules.

Owner / administrators

The owner is the current administrator. Admin powers must be used for platform operations, safety, security and documented moderation—not personal retaliation.

Moderators

No separate moderator role is represented as active unless it is explicitly implemented with least-privilege permissions and an audit trail.

Service providers

Hosting, authentication, storage, analytics or media vendors process data only within their service roles and applicable agreements.

2. Content that is not allowed

  • Credible threats, targeted harassment, stalking, bullying, hateful attacks or encouragement of violence against people or groups.
  • Child sexual exploitation or abuse, grooming, sexualization of minors, non-consensual intimate imagery, sexual extortion or sharing intimate material without permission.
  • Doxxing, identity theft, publishing private contact details or sensitive personal information without lawful authority and consent.
  • Fraud, phishing, impersonation, fake giveaways, pyramid schemes, deceptive earning claims, spam, fake engagement and malicious redirects.
  • Malware, credential theft, unauthorized access, exploit distribution intended to harm, evasion of security controls or instructions to attack real systems.
  • Content that promotes dangerous acts likely to cause serious harm, or encourages self-harm. Educational, newsworthy, documentary or prevention context is considered carefully.
  • Illegal trade, prohibited goods or services, counterfeit products, stolen material, or regulated items offered without lawful authorization.
  • Copyright infringement, plagiarism, stolen media, or use of another person's work without permission or a valid legal basis.

3. Respectful participation

  • Criticize ideas and conduct, not a person's protected identity or private life.
  • No threats, repeated unwanted contact, brigading, coordinated abuse or deliberate disruption.
  • Do not impersonate SYK, another member, a public authority, a creator or a brand.
  • Keep posts relevant to the selected topic. Repeated promotional posting, referral spam and misleading links may be limited.
  • Respect language and cultural differences. Urdu and English content are welcome; abuse is not.

4. Knowledge, news and Wikipedia-inspired standards

For SYK knowledge articles, educational explainers and factual news summaries, use three principles inspired by Wikipedia's public editorial model:

  • Neutral point of view: describe significant perspectives fairly; distinguish fact, allegation, analysis and opinion.
  • Verifiability: cite reliable, identifiable sources for material factual claims, especially claims about living people, health, safety, politics or breaking events.
  • No unsupported original claims: do not present personal speculation, fabricated research, generated citations or unverified interpretation as established fact.

Show publication dates and link to original publishers where possible. Correct material errors visibly. Do not present SYK summaries as official Wikipedia content. Community opinions are opinions, not encyclopedia entries. Urgent or developing stories should be labelled as developing and updated when reliable evidence changes.

5. Copyright, attribution and creator rights

  • Upload only content you created, licensed, own, or are otherwise legally entitled to share.
  • Credit the creator and source when required by the licence; attribution alone does not replace permission.
  • Do not remove watermarks, ownership notices or technical rights information to disguise origin.
  • Use official embeds or links when a platform provides them. Do not download and re-upload third-party videos without permission.
  • SYK may restrict access to material after a credible copyright complaint and may request proof of ownership or authorization.

Copyright complaints should identify the work, the exact SYK URL, the complainant's authority and a way to contact them. A counter-notice or appeal may be submitted with evidence. Do not send sensitive identity documents unless requested through a secure, verified channel. No legal outcome is guaranteed by this policy page.

6. Privacy, accounts and security

  • Use your own account. Never share passwords, session tokens, one-time codes or private keys.
  • Collect only the personal information needed for a clear feature. Do not expose private member data in public feeds, URLs, logs or screenshots.
  • Respect post visibility settings and do not re-share private or followers-only content without permission.
  • Do not upload confidential documents or sensitive images unless the feature explicitly supports them and you understand who can access them.
  • Report account compromise promptly and use available account-security controls.

Private messaging and live/video services may have different technical privacy limits. Read the Privacy Policy; do not assume end-to-end encryption unless a feature explicitly confirms it.

7. AI-generated and manipulated material

  • Do not use AI to impersonate a real person, fabricate evidence, create non-consensual intimate content or target someone with deception.
  • Label realistic synthetic media when a reasonable viewer could mistake it for an authentic recording or event.
  • Do not invent references, expert endorsements, testimonials, statistics or news sources.
  • AI output must be reviewed by a human before publication in safety-critical, legal, financial, medical or high-impact contexts.

8. Discovery, recommendations and user control

  • Recommendations should be relevant, diverse and understandable—not based on hidden payment or fabricated engagement.
  • Paid placements and affiliate relationships must be clearly disclosed.
  • Users should be able to hide, report or leave content and adjust available interests or notification controls.
  • Do not use deceptive interface patterns, forced autoplay with sound, fake urgency, infinite notification pressure or misleading counters.
  • Do not promise reach, views, income or ranking. Recommendations are not guarantees.

9. Monetization, jobs and offers

  • No guaranteed earnings, fake balances, fake job listings, fake discounts or fabricated audience metrics.
  • Clearly disclose ads, affiliate links, sponsorships, eligibility conditions and material conflicts of interest.
  • Never ask members to pay an upfront fee to unlock a promised job or guaranteed earning unless a legitimate, clearly documented service is involved and lawful.
  • Creators are responsible for rights, tax obligations and compliance with applicable laws and third-party partner rules.

10. Reporting, review and appeals

  1. Use the in-product Report control where it exists, and include the exact post, video, profile or resource URL plus a concise reason.
  2. Reports should be made in good faith. False, retaliatory or coordinated reports may themselves be abusive.
  3. Reviewers assess the content, context, severity, repetition, evidence and applicable policy. Automated signals may assist but should not be treated as infallible proof.
  4. Possible actions include no action, a warning, removal, reach restriction, feature restriction, temporary suspension or account termination, depending on severity and available controls.
  5. Where practical and safe, the affected member should receive the rule category and a reason. Severe cases may require immediate protective action.
  6. Members may appeal a moderation action by replying through the available official SYK support/report route with the decision reference and relevant evidence. A different reviewer should handle an appeal where staffing permits.
Implementation transparency: this page publishes the policy baseline. It does not by itself create a working report queue, moderator role, automated detection, appeal inbox, age-verification system or copyright form. Those controls must be implemented and tested in the application and database before SYK can claim they are operational.

11. Enforcement and transparency

Moderation should be proportionate and consistent. Reviewers should record the rule category, action, time and reason while limiting access to sensitive report data. Members' private report details must not be exposed publicly. SYK should periodically review repeat abuse, mistaken removals, appeal outcomes, account security and policy gaps. A public transparency summary should use aggregate counts only when those counts are actually measured.

12. External services and platform-specific rules

When a member opens or embeds YouTube, TikTok, WhatsApp, Facebook, X, Wikipedia or another external service, that provider's own terms, community rules, copyright requirements, age limits, API rules and privacy policy apply to that service. SYK cannot override those rules or grant permission to copy their content. SYK's own rules apply to material submitted or hosted in SYK-controlled areas.

13. Policy changes and contact

Rules may be updated as features, laws and safety risks change. Material changes should be dated and communicated clearly. If a report, copyright complaint or appeal feature is not available in the app, do not assume that a message has been received; use only a contact route visibly published by SYK.
