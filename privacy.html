PRIVACY INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

SYK Cyber Hub is a public digital directory with an owner-only administration area. Public visitors may create free member accounts or sign in. The SK Pulse community lets signed-in members publish posts, upload photos, choose post visibility, like, comment, follow other members, save posts and report abuse. The website does not currently process payments. The administrator may sign in to manage app and resource listings. Members may register and sign in using Supabase Auth; the database assigns new accounts the member role. Member accounts do not have administrator permissions.

Hosting and technical information

The website is published through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as IP address, browser details and request logs to deliver and secure the site. Please review GitHub's privacy documentation for details of its processing.

Supabase administration and database

If the owner configures Supabase, the site uses Supabase services for administrator authentication and the app catalogue. The Supabase project URL and public anon/publishable key are stored in this browser's local storage as configuration. The Supabase client may store an administrator authentication session in browser storage. Administrator email/account information and the administrator role are processed by Supabase Auth and the profiles table. App listing details are stored in the apps table. The website is designed to verify the administrator role before showing management controls. Never enter a Supabase service-role key or database password into the website.

Browser storage is specific to a browser and device. Clearing site data or using another browser/device may remove the saved configuration or session. The owner is responsible for the Supabase project's access policies, Row Level Security, retention and account security.

Community posts, profiles and uploaded photos

When the owner activates the SK Community database setup, member display names, profile biographies, public posts, comments, likes, follows, saved-post records and abuse reports are stored in the SYK Cyber Hub Supabase database. Public posts and profile display information may be visible to visitors. Photos uploaded to the community are stored in the platform's shared Supabase Storage bucket under a separate user-ID folder; members cannot upload into or delete another member's folder. The bucket is private: signed image URLs are issued only when the related post is visible to that viewer. Photos on public posts can be viewed by anyone; photos on followers-only posts are available only to permitted followers. Each member has a 50 MB photo cap, but all members still share the project's overall free storage and bandwidth quotas. Version 1 accepts JPEG, PNG and WebP images up to 8 MB; video files are not uploaded, but members may share external video links. Uploaded images are compressed in the browser where supported. Do not upload highly sensitive photos. The owner should monitor the shared free storage and bandwidth quotas. Members may delete their own posts; removal of a post does not guarantee copies already downloaded or cached elsewhere are erased.

Language selection and translation

The site language selector uses Google Translate functionality. Choosing a language may set a Google Translate preference cookie (commonly named googtrans) and send page text to Google's translation service. The news translation feature may send selected headlines or summaries to Google Translate or MyMemory translation services. Their own privacy terms apply.

News search and external data services

When you search for news, the search topic is used to construct RSS feed requests. To retrieve feeds, the browser may contact third-party services such as AllOrigins and RSS2JSON. The site may also request public image information from Wikimedia Commons. These services may receive your IP address, browser information and the requested feed or search terms. News results link to their original publishers; we do not control those publishers' websites.

Voice input

If you use the news voice-search button and your browser supports speech recognition, audio processing is handled by the browser and its speech-recognition provider. Availability and processing practices depend on your browser, device and provider. You can type the search instead.

Files and imports

The admin bulk-import feature reads selected CSV or Excel files in the browser and submits listing data to the configured Supabase database after the administrator confirms the import. The selected spreadsheet file itself is not intended to be stored as a public download by this website. Do not import personal or sensitive information.

Internet connection, bandwidth and personal devices

SYK Cyber Hub is hosted by cloud services and does not run from the owner's phone, laptop or personal internet connection. The owner's device and internet connection are not required for visitors to use the published website. SYK Cyber Hub does not turn a visitor's device or internet connection into a proxy, bandwidth-sharing node, relay or background network service. Visitors are not paid or charged based on their internet-data usage by SYK Cyber Hub. Standard internet or mobile-data charges, if any, are set by the visitor's own network provider.

When private messaging is enabled, message text is stored in the SYK Cyber Hub Supabase database and access is restricted to the two conversation participants under database policies. This version is not end-to-end encrypted; do not send passwords, financial details or sensitive secrets.

When one-to-one calls are enabled, audio/video may travel directly between participants using WebRTC. Network conditions may require a relay; no paid TURN relay is configured in the free-first pilot. Screen sharing depends on browser/device support.

When SK Live is enabled, live audio/video is transported through LiveKit Cloud to deliver the stream to viewers. LiveKit processes the media stream as a service provider. SK Live does not currently record or save live video to the SK community photo storage bucket. LiveKit free-plan quotas apply; live sessions may stop accepting new participants after the free limit is reached. The owner will not enable a paid upgrade without explicit approval.

SK Browser Android app (WebView)

On Android 9 and older, the app may request legacy storage permission only when you start a file download, so it can save that file to the public Downloads folder. On newer Android versions, the system download service handles the destination without that legacy permission. The file picker opens only after you select an upload control and choose a file.

The SYK Cyber Hub Android app includes a native Android WebView browser. Websites and Google Search result pages are displayed inside the SK app. When you type into the browser address/search field, partial search text may be sent to Google's search-suggestion service to show live suggestions; submitting a search sends the full query to Google. Opening a website sends the requested URL and normal connection information to that website. The browser keeps a limited browsing history on the device until you choose Clear all history. The clear-history action removes the app's local history and SK Browser's saved search-history key; it does not erase records held by Google, websites, your network provider or Android. Downloaded files are saved through Android DownloadManager to the device's Downloads folder. When you select a file in a website upload form, the file is shared with that website only after you choose it. The app blocks cleartext HTTP browsing where possible, upgrades ordinary HTTP page links to HTTPS, blocks mixed content and cancels invalid TLS certificates. Some external app actions such as telephone, email or Play Store links may open another app. Standard mobile-data or Wi-Fi charges may apply through your network provider.

Third-party links and libraries

Resource cards link to external providers. The page also loads required libraries and translation components from third-party content-delivery networks. When your browser loads or opens these services, their providers may receive technical information and apply their own cookies or policies. Check each provider's terms before using it.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

Language preferences and Supabase authentication may use browser storage or cookies as described above. No separate advertising network, affiliate tracking integration or analytics platform is currently active in this release. If these services are introduced, this policy will be updated and any required notices or consent controls will be provided.

Children and safety

Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of external services and check age requirements and content ratings.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the platform gains features. The date above will be revised when material changes are made.
