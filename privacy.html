PRIVACY INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

SK Cyber Hub is a public digital directory with an owner-only administration area. Public visitors do not currently create member accounts or make payments through this website. The administrator may sign in to manage app and resource listings.

Hosting and technical information

The website is published through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as IP address, browser details and request logs to deliver and secure the site. Please review GitHub's privacy documentation for details of its processing.

Supabase administration and database

If the owner configures Supabase, the site uses Supabase services for administrator authentication and the app catalogue. The Supabase project URL and public anon/publishable key are stored in this browser's local storage as configuration. The Supabase client may store an administrator authentication session in browser storage. Administrator email/account information and the administrator role are processed by Supabase Auth and the profiles table. App listing details are stored in the apps table. The website is designed to verify the administrator role before showing management controls. Never enter a Supabase service-role key or database password into the website.

Browser storage is specific to a browser and device. Clearing site data or using another browser/device may remove the saved configuration or session. The owner is responsible for the Supabase project's access policies, Row Level Security, retention and account security.

Language selection and translation

The site language selector uses Google Translate functionality. Choosing a language may set a Google Translate preference cookie (commonly named googtrans) and send page text to Google's translation service. The news translation feature may send selected headlines or summaries to Google Translate or MyMemory translation services. Their own privacy terms apply.

News search and external data services

When you search for news, the search topic is used to construct RSS feed requests. To retrieve feeds, the browser may contact third-party services such as AllOrigins and RSS2JSON. The site may also request public image information from Wikimedia Commons. These services may receive your IP address, browser information and the requested feed or search terms. News results link to their original publishers; we do not control those publishers' websites.

Voice input

If you use the news voice-search button and your browser supports speech recognition, audio processing is handled by the browser and its speech-recognition provider. Availability and processing practices depend on your browser, device and provider. You can type the search instead.

Files and imports

The admin bulk-import feature reads selected CSV or Excel files in the browser and submits listing data to the configured Supabase database after the administrator confirms the import. The selected spreadsheet file itself is not intended to be stored as a public download by this website. Do not import personal or sensitive information.

Third-party links and libraries

Resource cards link to external providers. The page also loads required libraries and translation components from third-party content-delivery networks. When your browser loads or opens these services, their providers may receive technical information and apply their own cookies or policies. Check each provider's terms before using it.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

Language preferences and Supabase authentication may use browser storage or cookies as described above. No separate advertising network, affiliate tracking integration or analytics platform is currently active in this release. If these services are introduced, this policy will be updated and any required notices or consent controls will be provided.

Children and safety

Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of external services and check age requirements and content ratings.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the platform gains features. The date above will be revised when material changes are made.
